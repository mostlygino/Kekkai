Kekkai

結界 · a barrier for your Mac

Kekkai shows every app that talks to the network, where it goes and how much it sends, and stops the ones you don't trust. Everything it sees stays on this Mac.

  • Watch live connections per app, with countries on a world map.
  • Block domains, addresses and apps with macOS's own firewall.
  • ⌘K to find any app, host or action.

This installs Kekkai.app in your Applications folder. It needs macOS 26 or later.

Blocking one app exactly, and asking before new apps connect, use Kekkai's network filter. It loads only in a copy signed by a developer team; this one runs Watch mode and the system firewall.
